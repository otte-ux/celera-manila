Our Story

Worldly but Welcoming

A play on selera, the Malay word for 'appetite,' CELERA layers and refines flavors accumulated over two decades of Asian cooking, culminating in a cuisine d'auteur. Worldly but welcoming, CELERA seeks to create a distinctive fine dining experience in Manila.
